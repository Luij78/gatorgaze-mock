Before we arrive

Photography pre-shoot checklist

A quick checklist based on years of photographing homes across Tampa Bay. Please finish it before the photographer arrives.

Your appointment is reserved for a set block of time, and delays can affect the appointments that follow. When we arrive, the property must be completely ready: all lights on, all blinds and curtains open, and all ceiling fans off. No one should be inside the home when the shoot starts, including pets, unless absolutely necessary, and no cars may be parked in the driveway. There is no fee to reschedule or cancel, but please let us know at least 24 hours before your appointment.

Questions? Call or text (813) 415-6349 or email info@gatorgazemedia.com.

General

  • Declutter each room so it reads as spacious
  • Turn on all lights and replace burnt-out bulbs
  • Open curtains and blinds
  • Clean floors, countertops, windows, and mirrors
  • Hide cords, trash bins, pet items, and personal belongings
  • Remove family photos and bold décor for a neutral look
  • Set the thermostat to a comfortable temperature

Living room

  • Fluff pillows and straighten cushions
  • Clear coffee tables and bookshelves
  • Hide remotes, toys, and extra accessories
  • A small plant or throw blanket is enough styling

Bedrooms

  • Make the beds and smooth the duvet
  • Clear nightstands down to a lamp and one small object
  • Close closet doors and hide laundry
  • Open blinds and turn lamps on

Kitchen

  • Clear countertops of appliances and clutter
  • Wipe stainless steel and reflective surfaces
  • Remove dish racks, soap, sponges, and towels
  • Hide trash cans and pet bowls

Bathrooms

  • Close toilet lids
  • Remove toothbrushes, razors, and toiletries
  • Hang clean, neutral towels
  • Wipe mirrors and counters

Outdoor and curb appeal

  • Mow, trim, and pick up yard debris
  • Sweep porches, patios, and the entry
  • Move vehicles out of the driveway
  • Hide hoses, tools, and bins
