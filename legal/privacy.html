Privacy Policy

This is a demo mockup, not the privacy policy of Gator Gaze Media. Do not submit real personal information.

The order form and the contact forms store what you type in this browser only, using session storage for the order draft. Nothing is posted to a server. Closing the tab or choosing “Start a new demo order” clears the draft. Map tiles may be requested from OpenStreetMap when the order form loads; the address you type is not sent with those requests.

The real studio’s policy lives on gatorgazemedia.com. This page exists so footer links in the mock have somewhere to go.
