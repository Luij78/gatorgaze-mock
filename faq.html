FAQ

Your questions, our answers

Straight answers on services, timing, travel, and how a shoot actually runs.

What services does Gator Gaze Media offer?

Photo and video for real estate listings (photos, drone, floor plans, Zillow 3D tours, Matterport, reels, cinematic video, virtual staging, and twilight), Airbnb and VRBO rentals, commercial spaces, events, business social content, and agent branding reels and headshots.

Which areas do you serve?

Tampa, South Tampa, Brandon, Valrico, Lutz, Wesley Chapel, St. Petersburg and the Pinellas beaches, Bradenton, Anna Maria Island, Longboat Key, Sarasota, Siesta Key, Spring Hill, Lakeland, Orlando, and Kissimmee. For other nearby cities, reach out for a custom quote.

How can I book a photography session?

Choose a service on the booking page and place the order online. You can also call (813) 415-6349 or email info@gatorgazemedia.com.

What is the turnaround time?

Edited photos are delivered within 24 hours of the shoot. Videos and virtual services are typically delivered within 48–72 hours. Photos are ready by 10:00 AM the morning after the shoot.

Do you offer same-day services?

Urgent requests are accommodated when the schedule allows. Contact the studio directly to check same-day availability. Event same-day delivery is a $150 add-on.

Do you work with both residential and commercial properties?

Yes. The same team covers residential listings, commercial spaces, vacation rentals, and events.

How do I prepare my property?

Have the property completely ready on arrival: all lights on, all blinds and curtains open, and all ceiling fans off. No one should be inside when the shoot starts, including pets, unless absolutely necessary, and no cars may be parked in the driveway. The Home Prep Guide has the full checklist.

What payment methods do you accept?

Major credit cards and bank transfers. Payment is typically due when the final files are delivered. No deposit is required.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

There is no fee to reschedule or cancel. Please send word at least 24 hours before the appointment.

How will I receive my photos and videos?

Through a private online gallery where you can view, download, and share. Galleries include MLS-ready and high-resolution versions. Packages also include a property website.

Can you add community or amenity photos?

Yes. Pools, clubhouses, and neighborhood entrances can be added. Community photos are $30 on a photo-only order and included in Standard and above.

Do you provide virtual staging or twilight edits?

Yes. Virtual staging is $25 per image and virtual twilight is $15 per image on residential orders. Real twilight is 5 photos for $200.

Do you offer video walkthroughs or reels?

Vertical social reels start at $175 and cinematic listing videos are $350. Agent on camera is $100.

Can you include drone lot lines or map pins?

Yes. Digital lot lines and pins are a $10 residential add-on and are included in Standard packages and above.

Who do you work with?

Realtors, builders, investors, property managers, and homeowners. Each shoot is set up around the listing or the brand.

What if the weather is bad?

If rain or the sky will not serve the property, the shoot can be rescheduled at no extra cost. The Blue Sky Guarantee covers sky replacement on delivered photos when a reshoot is not the better call.

Do you offer weekend shoots?

Seven days a week, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, subject to availability.

How long does a typical shoot take?

Most photo shoots take 45–90 minutes depending on size and add-ons. Larger homes and video days run longer.

Can I be present during the shoot?

To keep the appointment on time, the home should be empty when the shoot starts, including pets, unless that is impossible. Lockbox access is welcome.

Do you shoot events?

Yes. Parties, mixers, fundraisers, and corporate events. Event video starts at $500, photography at $400, and photo plus video at $800, all with a 2-hour minimum.

What does Agent Branding include?

Vertical reels edited with music, logo, and captions. An intro reel is $295, 3 reels are $650, 6 reels are $1,150, and 10 reels plus a bio video are $1,750. Headshots are $150 and booked separately.

How do reel package locations work?

3 reels cover up to 2 locations, 6 reels up to 3, and 10 reels up to 4. An extra location is $100.

Do you create social content for businesses?

Yes. Single videos from $350, plus starter, growth, and pro packages, and monthly plans from $1,100.

What is an AI video edit?

An optional $50-per-scene add-on for a more polished look, smoother transitions, or visual effects. A vacant-land-to-property AI video is $350.

Do you photograph vacant land?

Yes. $150 for a mix of 15 drone and ground photos. A one-minute drone video and an AI property video can be added.

Why are Airbnb, commercial, and event shoots priced differently?

MLS listing photos are licensed for listing marketing. Airbnb, commercial, event, business, and agent-branding work is licensed for broader advertising, so it is priced separately.

What is the difference between Airbnb and MLS photography?

Airbnb and VRBO photography has no photo limit and includes an extended usage license, drone photos, lot lines, community photos, and virtual twilight. MLS photos follow the package photo counts.

Do you offer a Zillow 3D Tour for commercial spaces?

A commercial Zillow 3D Tour is $200 up to 2,500 sq ft and $225 above that. Commercial Matterport starts at $275.

How much do agent headshots cost in Tampa?

$150 for a 30-minute on-location session, one look, and 3 retouched photos within 48 hours.

How much do business portraits cost?

$150 per person, on location. A team spotlight video is $400 and can be filmed on the same visit.

What does a monthly video package cost?

$1,100 for 4 videos, $2,000 for 8, and $2,800 for 12, from one scheduled shoot. Logo and captions are $50.

How much do interview and testimonial videos cost?

An interview is $550, a testimonial is $450, a team spotlight is $400, and a brand or promo video is $900.

How much is the travel fee outside Tampa?

The first 30 miles from South Tampa are free. After that it is $15 for every 10 miles, rounded down to the nearest 10 miles, on the one-way distance.

Do you provide branding or marketing materials?

Packages include a branded and unbranded property website. A logo, headshot, or contact line can be added to the gallery.
