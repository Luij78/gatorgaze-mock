Your questions, our answers

All FAQs
What services does Gator Gaze Media offer?

Photo and video for real estate listings (photos, drone, floor plans, Zillow 3D tours, Matterport, reels, cinematic video, virtual staging, and twilight), Airbnb and VRBO rentals, commercial spaces, events, business social content, and agent branding reels and headshots.

Which areas do you serve?

Tampa, South Tampa, Brandon, Valrico, Lutz, Wesley Chapel, St. Petersburg and the Pinellas beaches, Bradenton, Anna Maria Island, Longboat Key, Sarasota, Siesta Key, Spring Hill, Lakeland, Orlando, and Kissimmee. For other nearby cities, reach out for a custom quote.

How can I book a photography session?

Choose a service on the booking page and place the order online. You can also call (813) 415-6349 or email info@gatorgazemedia.com.

What is the turnaround time?

Edited photos are delivered within 24 hours of the shoot. Videos and virtual services are typically delivered within 48–72 hours. Photos are ready by 10:00 AM the morning after the shoot.

Do you offer same-day services?

Urgent requests are accommodated when the schedule allows. Contact the studio directly to check same-day availability. Event same-day delivery is a $150 add-on.

Do you work with both residential and commercial properties?

Yes. The same team covers residential listings, commercial spaces, vacation rentals, and events.
